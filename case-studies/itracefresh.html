Getting To Know Our Users And Competitors

In The Field With Harvest Crews

Spent a day on-site at a California Giant Berry Farm observing and interviewing harvest and operations teams to understand the real-world workflow.

Who We Were Designing For

Our customers' pain points were our opportunities. Our interviews helped us uncover the path to our goals.

What The Journey Mapping Revealed

Workflow mapping revealed two major time sinks:

  • 5 hrs — Packing & prepping product
  • 1.5 hrs — Palletizing & labeling

These tasks consumed 6.5 hours of the shift, exposing significant workflow friction. A lack of real-time visibility left crews guessing about supplies, progress, and quotas—while operations teams lacked timely insight into production and quality issues.

What The Competitive Landscape Confirmed

iTracefresh wasn't behind on capability. It held features most competitors didn't but those solutions were scattered across separate, disconnected modules, while competitors like Procurant and Famous ERP won on being one unified system.

Translating Data & Research Into A End To End Solution

Improved Information Architecture

iTracefresh modules are now all accessible within the side navigation.

Improved findability for creating labels by 37%.

Streamlining The Label Creation In One Flow

Designed a guided wizard to help users quickly progress through the setup.

Step progress bar will be anchored to each step to show progression.

User will automatically progress to next step after filling out required fields in each step. Reducing clicks by 58%.

Personalizing The Scan Experience For Every Brand

Growers wanted their own branding to carry through past the warehouse, all the way to the moment a customer engages with the product.

Growers can personalize their own branded marketing pages to help customers connect with the brand.

Self-service template configuration for both web and mobile experiences.

Preventing Disasters With A Recall Management Event

Growers wanted a solution where they can quickly respond to food-borne illnesses or contaminations by creating a recall event.

Based on feedback, stores and customers will now be notified when growers initiate a recall event.

Growers can manage all recall events in one centralized platform.
